# -SmartBudgetManagger-
A project for managing Budget
SmartBudget Manager 
SmartBudget Manager is an Excel file for keeping track of your money each month. 
You type in what you earn and what you spend, and the file works out your balance 
for you. It also tells you when you have spent more than your budget. 
What you need 
● Microsoft Excel 2016 or newer (2019, 2021 and Microsoft 365 also work) 
● The file SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx 
You do not need an internet connection and there is nothing to install. 
What is in the file 
The workbook has three worksheets. You can switch between them using the tabs at 
the bottom of the Excel window. 
● Dashboard: shows your total income, total expenses, balance, a pie chart of 
your spending, and a message telling you if you are within your budget. 
● Transactions: the place where you enter your income and your expenses. 
● Categories: the list of expense categories (Food, Transport, Rent, Health, 
Education) with a monthly budget for each one. 
How to start 
1. Open SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx. If Excel shows a yellow bar at the top, click 
Enable Editing. 
2. Open the Categories worksheet and type the monthly budget you want for 
each category. 
3. Open the Transactions worksheet. In the first empty row, fill in the Date, the 
Description, the Type (Income or Expense) and the Category. 
4. Type the amount in the Income column if it is money you received, or in the 
Expense column if it is money you spent. 
5. Open the Dashboard worksheet to see your balance, the chart and the budget 
message. 
6. Press Ctrl + S to save. 
Things to remember 
● Pick the Type and the Category from the drop-down list. Do not type them 
yourself. 
● Do not change the Month column. It fills in by itself. 
● Do not delete cells that contain formulas. 
The budget message compares your total expenses with your total budget. It does 
not warn you about one category on its own.
